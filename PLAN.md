# A 14-day plan that works

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0 | Create the closed track. Add testers through a Google Group. Share the opt-in link **and** the Play Store link. |
| 1 to 3 | Check in Play Console that every tester shows as opted in. Chase anyone missing. Collect first impressions. |
| 4 to 10 | Fix the first real bugs and ship an update. Ask testers to update and keep using the app. |
| 11 to 14 | Ship a second small update if you can. Write down what was tested, on which devices, what feedback you got and what you changed. |
| 15 | Apply for production access with those notes. |

## Keep a simple log during the test

| Date | Tester / phone | What they reported | What you changed | Version |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

On day 15, this log is most of your production access answers.

## What testers on real phones typically catch

- An amount or button label that wraps onto two lines on small screens.
- A system dialog in English inside an app that is otherwise in another language.
- "1 addresses": a plural never tested with a single item.
- A demo video or stream that fails on some brands.
- A blank screen at startup on phones with an outdated WebView.
- A Play Store short description that does not say what the app does.
