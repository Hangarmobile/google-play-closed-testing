# Resources

## Official (Google)

- [App testing requirements for new personal developer accounts](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465): the 12 testers / 14 days rule and the production access form.
- [Set up an open, closed or internal test](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334): how to create the closed track and add testers.
- [Android vitals](https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/vitals): crash rate and ANR rate, and why they matter for visibility.
- [Google Play Developer Community](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/community): official help forum.

## Finding testers for free

- Friends, family and colleagues: ask them to open the app every day or two, not just to opt in.
- Tester exchange communities on Reddit, Discord and Telegram: you test other developers' apps, they test yours. Search Reddit for "12 testers" or "closed testing".
- Your existing audience: newsletter, waitlist, social followers.

## Paid services

Several services provide 12+ testers for the full 14 days. Check that they use real physical devices, that testers actually use the app, and whether you get feedback during the test.

- [PlayTesterHub](https://playtesterhub.com/en): 12 real Android phones in France, tips during the test. *Disclosure: run by the maintainers of this repository.*
- [Testers Community](https://www.testerscommunity.com)
- [Twelve Testers](https://twelvetesters.com)
- Freelance marketplaces such as Fiverr.
