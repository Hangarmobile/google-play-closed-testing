# Checklist before applying for production

- [ ] Personal developer account created after Nov 13, 2023 (otherwise the rule does not apply).
- [ ] The test runs on a **closed** track, not internal testing.
- [ ] 14 to 15 testers recruited, to stay safely above 12 if someone drops out.
- [ ] Testers received both the **opt-in link** and the **Play Store link**.
- [ ] At least 12 testers opted in for the **last 14 consecutive days**.
- [ ] Testers actually open and use the app, not just opt in.
- [ ] You collected feedback (form, chat, messages) and kept notes.
- [ ] At least one new build shipped during the test, based on that feedback.
- [ ] Android vitals checked: crash rate and ANR rate.
- [ ] Your answers to the production access form are written with real numbers (see [PRODUCTION-ACCESS.md](PRODUCTION-ACCESS.md)).
